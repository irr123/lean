# Lean transfer

Write a mode-600 `$TMPDIR/handoff-<YYYYmmdd-HHMM>.md`. Cite sources, reference artifact paths, redact secrets and PII. Report its absolute path.

Document structure:
1. **Session log path**: Absolute path.
2. **Scope**: Problem and targets.
3. **Status**: Progress and current state.
4. **Evidence**: Verified facts and artifact paths, separate from assumptions.
5. **Decisions**: Choices and rationale.
6. **Open questions**: Blockers and gaps.
7. **Next actions**: Immediate steps.
